This is an incomplete research development for JSP-000404 / Erdős 504.

- The underlying historical mathematics is attributed to Szekeres,
  Erdős–Szekeres, and Blagovest Sendov; the classification is not our discovery.
- OpenAI Codex prepared the code, proof attempts, diagnostics, and local
  verification at the direction of the user operating GitHub account
  **Jackie1021**. The account holder requested the investigation and publication;
  this does not assert that they personally wrote or independently reviewed each
  proof step. The project is human-directed, AI-assisted research.
- Prior public PRs 42, 100, 300 and 647 are linked in README.md. Their reported
  partial results and the previously reported concern with Sendov's capacity
  argument are prior work. No priority for discovering that concern is claimed.
- The project's Lean source imports Mathlib and its own modules. It does not
  import the third-party Lean sources inspected during research. Those sources
  are not copied into this repository.
- Mathematical and formalization attribution remain separate. A successful
  local build does not establish originality, completeness, first-formalization
  priority, official acceptance, or award entitlement.

The private research files predate this public repository. Git timestamps and
old local logs are not independent public completion evidence. In particular,
this repository does not yet contain a complete proof of the original problem.

address and explicitly describe the AI assistance. This is not organizer
identity verification. There is no independent human reviewer in this workflow.
